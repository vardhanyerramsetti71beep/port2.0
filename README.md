## ⚡ Highlights & Key Features

- **Chunky 3D Paper Money Gun (Inline SVG)**:
  - Angled isometric red cash-cannon with multi-tone face gradients (`#ff5a5e` top, `#ff3b3f` side, `#7d050a` rear).
  - Recoil animation (kicks back 14px on each shot and springs forward).
  - Mechanical trigger rotation upon firing.
  - White bold `PORTFOLIO` side branding.
- **Physics-Driven Bill Flutter Simulation**:
  - Slips spawn from the gun's dark muzzle slot coordinate.
  - Realistic trajectory ($150^\circ - 190^\circ$, gravity $+0.28$, horizontal drag $\times 0.975$, fall speed capped at $5$).
  - Dynamic sine-wave sideways flutter and $\pm 55^\circ$ bill flip (`rotateX`).
  - Seamless $0.7\text{s}$ ease-out glide into the docked tray.
- **Interactive Tray & Twin Hop Mechanism**:
  - Slips dock neatly into organized rows with $10\text{px}$ gaps.
  - Duplicate slips fly directly onto their twin, fade out, and trigger an $8\text{px}$ hop bounce animation on the docked twin.
  - Hover lift $6\text{px}$ with rich drop shadows.
- **Tactile Keycap Controls**:
  - Mechanical keyboard keycap aesthetics (54px height, 14px radius, 7px darker 3D base bevel).
  - Sink-and-spring `:active` depression.
  - Legend markers (`⏎` for spray, `↺` for clean up, `@` for contact).
  - Hold button, click & hold the gun, or hold `Space` to spray at $85\text{ms}$ intervals.
- **Web Audio API Synthesizer**:
  - Zero-latency procedural sound generation (flickering paper flutter, ratchet clicks, landing snaps).
  - Mute/Unmute audio toggle button.
- **Verified Production Work Included**:
  1. **Scalable Task & Data Management Cloud Portal** (Flask, React, MySQL, AWS EC2/S3, Docker)
  2. **OmniPulse Enterprise CLV & Retention Engine** (FastAPI, Scikit-learn, BG/NBD, Gamma-Gamma)
  3. **Facial Expression Music Recommendation System** (Flask, OpenCV, Scikit-learn, WebRTC)
  4. **90-Day Body Transformation Biometric 3D Manifolds** (Plotly 3D WebGL, Python)
  5. **S.V.T. Tailors Bespoke Atelier & 5-Stage Tracker** (Python Serverless, MySQL, React.js)
  6. **MS Pickles Artisanal Andhra E-Commerce Platform** (JavaScript ES6+, LocalStorage, Tailwind CSS)
